# Slate visual identity

Slate uses one family mark across R1, R2, the website, documentation, and release communication: a calm cat-fox holding a cobalt fountain pen, framed by an ember flame tail.

The mark represents:

- **Cat-fox:** curiosity, focus, and adaptability.
- **Fountain pen:** deliberate writing and document craft.
- **Flame tail:** ideas in motion and the distinctive spark of Slate.

R1 and R2 remain independent products. The shared mark identifies the Slate family; product names, interface colors, package identities, repositories, and release channels identify the individual products.

## Primary assets

| Asset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [slate-mascot-primary.png](assets/slate-mascot-primary.png) | Lossless transparent master for documentation and release graphics |

The mascot is a free-standing mark. It does not require a border, badge, circle, rounded square, or decorative container.

## Core colors

| Name | Hex | Purpose |

| Ink navy | `#172033` | Primary text and dark mascot structure |
| CORE cobalt | `#145CFF` | Fountain pen and primary action accent |
| Ember | `#FF533D` | Flame-tail signature accent |
| Paper | `#FFF6E5` | Mascot highlights and warm contrast |

## Usage

- Preserve the complete cat-fox, pen, and flame-tail silhouette.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one ear width.
- Use the PNG master for compositing and the WebP asset on the web.
- Keep the mark at least 40 px wide in screen layouts.
- Place it on quiet light or dark backgrounds with sufficient contrast.
- Use the approved transparent mark as the adaptive-icon foreground over Slate midnight.

## Do not

- Add an outline, border, badge, or enclosing shape to the mascot.
- Restore stone, metallic, glass, or photorealistic texture.
- remove the pen or flame tail.
- Stretch, rotate, crop, or rearrange the character.
- Add small product text or numerals inside the mark.
- Substitute generic document, page, or note imagery.

The Android launcher treatment is an application of the family mark, not the definition of the brand itself.
