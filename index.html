A document, start to finish

Pick up where
you left off.

  1. Give the draft a head start.

    Start with a report, letter, proposal, or creative brief. If the work already exists, open a supported Word, OpenDocument, HTML, or text file.

  2. Find the passage you need.

    Jump between headings with the outline, update repeated wording with find and replace, and use document statistics to track a writing goal.

  3. Check the finished pages.

    Open page preview before printing or saving as PDF. Export an editable copy when someone else needs to keep working on it.

Make it yours

Set the mood
for writing.

Pick an editor theme or follow your system appearance. Change the accent, switch on the dark page view, or fit the sheet to your window.

Dark page view is a screen preference. Your document’s original colors carry through to printing and exports.

On-device saving

Your work stays
with you.

Slate Forge saves your work in this browser on this device. Export a workspace backup when you want to move to another device or keep a separate copy. You can password-protect the backup.

Clearing browser data can remove your saved workspace. Keep an independent backup of important work.
