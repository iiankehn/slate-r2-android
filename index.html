A REAL WORD PROCESSOR

More than notes.
Made for documents.

Slate R2 combines a familiar ribbon and paginated canvas with an interface that scales naturally across every supported Android form factor.

01

Page-aware editing

Write across stacked pages with margins, columns, custom sizes, headers, footers, and continuous or page-based sections.

02

Rich document tools

Format text, build multilevel lists, toggle checklists, edit and merge table cells, and place pictures with text wrapping.

03

DOCX, PDF, and print

Import and export structured Word documents, preserve embedded pictures, create PDFs, and print from Android.

04

Recovery built in

Versioned local storage, bounded recovery checkpoints, undo and redo keep your work resilient without an account.
