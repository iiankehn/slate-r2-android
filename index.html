Browser word processor · Public beta

Make room for words.

Draft in a continuous editor, shape your work on the page, and take it with you as a Word document or PDF. Slate Forge runs in your browser, with no account required.

Open Slate Forge ↗Explore the workspace

The workspace

From first draft to finished page.

Start with a blank document or a template. Switch views as your work moves from drafting to layout, then preview the pages before you print or share.

01

Write and revise

Format with headings, lists, and text styles. Navigate longer drafts with the outline, make edits with find and replace, or turn on focus mode.

02

Design the page

Switch between continuous and paged editing. Set margins and orientation, add headers and footers, wrap text around images, and style tables.

03

Work across formats

Import and export supported Word and OpenDocument files. Preview and print to PDF, annotate PDFs, or extract text from scans with OCR. Complex layouts can change during file conversion.

Local-first by design

Your browser is your workspace.

Slate Forge saves documents to this browser on this device. Export a workspace backup to move your work or keep a separate copy. Password-protected backups are available.

Keep a separate copy

Clearing browser data can remove your saved workspace. Download a backup before switching browsers or devices.
